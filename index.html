PI DECK

Privacy policy · updated 25 October 2026

What the game keeps

Your runs, scores, settings and weak-spot statistics are saved only on your device. PI DECK has no accounts and no servers, and we never receive this data. Deleting the app or tapping “Clear history” removes it.

Ads

The free versions of PI DECK on iPhone and Android show ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect information from your device, such as its advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and how you interact with ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google’s privacy policy.

Your choices

In the EU, UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice at any time in the app under History → Ad privacy settings. On iPhone, the game asks for permission to track only through Apple’s system prompt; you can change it in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.

Children

PI DECK is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
