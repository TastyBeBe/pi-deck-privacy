Eating Pi

Possible rewards · updated 31 October 2026

A random reward

Every completed roll gives one random reward. You unlock the selected pie if it is new to you. If you already own it, you receive 50 XP instead. You keep everything previous rolls gave you.

Every pie in the roll pool

  • Gummy Bear Pie
  • Cereal Pie
  • Pickle Pie
  • Hot Sauce Pie
  • Cream Pie
  • Pie-rate
  • Bad Pie
  • Poop Pie

Other pies unlock separately through play or specific ad offers; they are not in this random roll pool.

How to get rolls

Type your first 50 digits of the day without hints and you bank 2 rolls. Every 50 digits after that bank 1 more. The day follows your phone’s clock.

You can also choose to watch an ad for a Free roll, up to five a day.

After a roll, one optional Re-roll ad gives another random reward from the same pool. Your first reward stays yours. A Re-roll uses one of the day’s five Free rolls, and its result offers no further Re-roll.

An unavailable or unfinished ad gives no roll or reward. Rolls cannot be bought.
