Eating Pi

Possible rewards · updated 28 October 2026

A random reward

Every completed roll gives one random reward. You unlock the selected pie if it is new to you. If you already own it, you receive 50 XP instead. You keep everything previous rolls gave you.

Every pie in the roll pool

  • Gummy Bear Pie
  • Cereal Pie
  • Pickle Pie
  • Hot Sauce Pie
  • Cream Pie
  • Pie-rate
  • Bad Pie
  • Poop Pie

Other pies unlock separately through play or specific ad offers; they are not in this random roll pool.

How to get rolls

Earn one roll for every 30 digits typed with no hint showing. Your progress adds up across runs.

You may also choose to watch one ad to the end for a daily roll.

After a roll, one optional “Spin again” ad can give another random reward from the same pool. Your first reward stays yours. The extra spin does not offer another spin again.

An unavailable or unfinished ad gives no roll or reward. Rolls cannot be purchased.
